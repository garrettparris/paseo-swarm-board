# Swarm Board

Type `/swarm plan <brief>` for a product readback, independent Goldfish reviews, and a reviewed implementation plan. Type `/swarm run <brief>` to open a lead session and the shared board.

**Human Decisions** shows pending approvals and blocking decisions as they arrive.

The lead starts specialists and an independent verifier. Specialists can start workers. Named mentions queue follow-up turns, while the board keeps assignments, evidence, and review results together. Choose models per role in the daemon's `models.json` or ask for a member-specific override in the brief.

See [README.md](README.md) for setup, role settings, and the upstream Elephant & Goldfish skills.
